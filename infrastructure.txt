# Proposal: Digital Aggregators Partner Portal

## Purpose and Business Value

The Digital Aggregators Partner Portal is a secure, multi-tenant, service-oriented platform designed to enable digital aggregators and their staff to efficiently manage leads, documents, analytics, finance/payouts, training, and support. The platform provides:

- **Centralized Partner Engagement:** A single portal for partners to manage all operational workflows, reducing friction and improving productivity.
- **Lead Conversion Optimization:** Real-time analytics and funnel tracking to increase lead-to-loan conversion rates.
- **Automated Financial Workflows:** Streamlined payouts, invoices, commissions, and dispute resolution, reducing manual effort and errors.
- **Compliance and Security:** Strong RBAC, geo-scoping, audit logging, and AV scanning to meet regulatory and security requirements.
- **Scalable Integration:** API-first, event-driven architecture supporting REST, webhooks, and bulk integrations with partner IT systems.

### Business Objectives
- Enhance partner engagement and self-service capabilities.
- Increase lead conversion rates through detailed funnel analytics.
- Automate and streamline financial and compliance processes.
- Achieve a competitive edge by offering innovative digital solutions.
- Improve operational efficiency and data-driven decision-making.

---

## Scope

### In-Scope
- Lead intake, deduplication, enrichment, lifecycle management, and bulk/exchange.
- Secure document upload, storage, AV scanning, PII masking, and policy-driven access.
- Real-time analytics, reporting, funnel tracking, geo-analytics, and CQRS read models.
- Partner payouts, invoices, commissions, disputes, and tax documentation.
- User onboarding, RBAC, geo-scoping, session management, and access auditing.
- Support ticketing, escalation, SLA workflows, and announcements.
- Knowledge/training content, certifications, FAQs, and compliance resources.
- Centralized RBAC policy enforcement and immutable audit logging.
- Multi-channel notifications (in-app, email, SMS, webhooks).
- Event-driven async workflows and messaging/event streaming.
- Integration with external authentication, payment, AV scanning, notification, CDN, and analytics/monitoring systems.

### Out-of-Scope
- Core banking or loan origination system (LOS) logic (external integration only).
- Direct customer (end-borrower) facing features.
- Custom partner-specific UI branding (unless explicitly required).
- Legacy system migration (unless explicitly required).

---

## Responsibilities

- **API Gateway:** Unified ingress, authentication, RBAC, throttling, logging, and protocol mediation.
- **Lead Management:** Lead intake, deduplication, enrichment, lifecycle, and event publishing.
- **Document Management:** Secure upload/download, AV scanning, PII masking, and policy-driven access.
- **Analytics & Reporting:** Dashboards, funnel tracking, KPIs, geo-analytics, and CQRS read models.
- **Finance & Payouts:** Payouts, invoices, commissions, disputes, and tax documentation.
- **User & Access Management:** Onboarding, RBAC, geo-scoping, session management, and access auditing.
- **Ticketing & Communication:** Support tickets, escalation, SLA, and announcements.
- **Knowledge Hub:** Training, certifications, FAQs, and compliance content.
- **RBAC Infrastructure:** Centralized policy enforcement and permissions API.
- **Audit Logging:** Immutable audit trails for compliance and forensics.
- **Notification Service:** Multi-channel notifications and delivery tracking.
- **Messaging/Event Streaming:** Event brokering and async workflows.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Lead Management
- [ ] Partners can create, update, and view leads via `POST /leads`, `PUT /leads/{leadId}`, `GET /leads`, `GET /leads/{leadId}`.
- [ ] Bulk lead intake is supported via `POST /leads/bulk`.
- [ ] Lead events are published to the event bus for analytics and notifications.
- [ ] Lead data is partitioned by partner/tenant.

### Document Management
- [ ] Partners can upload documents via `POST /documents` with AV scanning and PII masking.
- [ ] Documents are stored securely in S3/Blob with pre-signed URL access via `GET /documents/{docId}/download`.
- [ ] Document metadata is retrievable via `GET /documents/{docId}`.
- [ ] All document actions are audit-logged.

### Analytics & Reporting
- [ ] Partners can view dashboards via `GET /analytics/dashboard`.
- [ ] Funnel and geo-analytics are available via `GET /analytics/funnel`, `GET /analytics/geo`.
- [ ] Reports can be generated/downloaded via `GET /analytics/reports`.

### Finance & Payouts
- [ ] Partners can view payouts and invoices via `GET /payouts`, `GET /invoices`.
- [ ] Disputes can be created and tracked via `POST /disputes`, `GET /disputes/{disputeId}`.
- [ ] Payout and invoice details are available via `GET /payouts/{payoutId}`, `GET /invoices/{invoiceId}`.

### User & Access Management
- [ ] Admins can onboard, update, and deactivate users via `POST /users`, `PUT /users/{userId}`, `DELETE /users/{userId}`.
- [ ] User permissions are retrievable via `GET /users/{userId}/permissions`.
- [ ] RBAC and geo-scoping are enforced on all API and UI actions.

### Ticketing & Communication
- [ ] Partners can create and track support tickets via `POST /tickets`, `GET /tickets`, `GET /tickets/{ticketId}`.
- [ ] Announcements can be created and listed via `POST /announcements`, `GET /announcements`.

### Knowledge Hub
- [ ] Partners can access articles and certifications via `GET /knowledge/articles`, `GET /knowledge/certifications`.

### Notifications
- [ ] Notifications are sent via in-app, email, SMS, and webhooks for platform events.
- [ ] Notification delivery is tracked via `GET /notifications`.

### Audit Logging
- [ ] All sensitive actions are logged to an immutable audit store.
- [ ] Audit logs are searchable via `GET /audit/logs`.

### Security & Compliance
- [ ] All APIs enforce OAuth2/JWT authentication and RBAC.
- [ ] Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
- [ ] System achieves 99.9% uptime SLA.
